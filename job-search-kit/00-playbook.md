## The uncomfortable finding first: your Security+ has to be retaken

Your Security+ ce expired **22 March 2026**. I checked CompTIA's current renewal policy so you are
not working from an assumption:

> "Candidates must pass the latest exam version to regain certification if not renewed."
> A **30-day grace period** applies to CEUs earned *within* the original three-year cycle.

That grace period closed in April 2026. **CEUs will not bring it back.** The only route to an active
Security+ is passing the current exam version.

This matters more than any project in this portfolio, because it is the one line on your résumé a
recruiter can verify in ten seconds — and right now it verifies as expired.

**Plan:**

| Step | Timing | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Confirm the current exam version on CompTIA's site (do not assume SY0-701 is still current) | Today | — |
| Book the exam 5–6 weeks out — a booked date is what makes you study | This week | ~$404 (voucher; check CompTIA discounts) |
| Study 8–10 h/week; you passed it once, this is revision not learning | 5 weeks | — |
| Pass, then immediately set a CEU tracking habit so this never happens again | — | $50/yr CE fee |

**On the résumé today:** "CompTIA Security+ (2023–2026, recertification exam scheduled [date])".
Honest, and it turns a red flag into evidence you are on top of your own credentials. Do not write
"renewal in progress" — that is now inaccurate, and inaccurate is the one thing you cannot afford.

**Second certification, cheap and fast:** you already hold ISC2 Candidate status. The CC exam is
short, inexpensive, and gives you an active certification while you retake Security+. Take it within
30 days.

## Your actual positioning

Do not compete as "entry-level GRC analyst." That pool has 200 applicants per opening, most with
2–3 years of experience.

Compete as: **"GRC analyst who does AI governance and understands federal contracting compliance."**

Three things make that credible, and only you have all three:

1. **AI governance with real evaluation experience.** Two years evaluating AI model outputs against
   safety and policy rubrics is the human-oversight layer NIST AI RMF and ISO 42001 require. Most
   GRC candidates have read about it. You did it.
2. **Federal contracting from the inside.** You run a SAM-registered, DBE/MBE-certified company. You
   understand SAM, UEI, DBE certification, and the compliance burden on subcontractors from the
   inside. NIST SP 800-171 and CMMC readiness is a large, underserved market.
3. **Bilingual EN/FR.** Relevant for any organisation with francophone West African, Canadian or
   European operations — including the mining and NGO sector you already know.

## Target list, in priority order

| Priority | Target | Why you |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **CPA firms doing federal audits** — GRF CPAs & Advisors, Williams Adley, and their peers | They audit federal grantees and contractors. You already have contacts. They hire junior IT auditors and value the contractor's-eye view |
| 2 | **Defense/federal subcontractors needing CMMC and 800-171 readiness** | Enormous demand, few candidates who understand both the compliance and the small-business reality |
| 3 | **GRC consultancies and MSSPs** | Volume hiring, project-based, portfolio matters more than tenure |
| 4 | **Healthcare and fintech in-house GRC** | Your HIPAA and PCI coverage fits; more competitive |
| 5 | **AI governance roles** | Small market, high pay, growing fast. Your differentiator is strongest here |

Set salary expectation at **$70–95K**, not $60–80K. Market data for 2026 puts GRC analyst at
$75–105K and IT auditor at $95–140K. Asking too low signals inexperience.

## Weekly rhythm

| Activity | Volume | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Targeted applications with a tailored résumé | 8–10/week | Tailored means the top 3 skill lines mirror the posting's language |
| Direct messages to hiring managers or GRC leads | 5/week | Ten targeted messages beat a hundred Indeed clicks |
| LinkedIn post | 1/week | You are building the case that you follow this field in real time |
| Interview practice | 1 session/week | Bring me a real job description and I will play the sceptical hiring manager |
| Portfolio work | 1 project/2 weeks | Keeps the material fresh and current |

Track everything in one sheet: company, role, date, channel, contact, status, next action. A job
search without a tracker becomes a feeling instead of a process, and feelings get discouraged around
week three.

## Expect this to take 3 to 6 months

You will be rejected repeatedly, often without a reply. That is the base rate of this market, not a
verdict on the work. The portfolio's job is to make sure that when you do reach a conversation, you
are the candidate they remember.
