## The five assessments

### [1 — AI Hiring System: Governance Assessment and Deployment Decision](p1-ai-hiring-assessment/)
An AI résumé screener that auto-rejects 4,400 candidates a year without human review, deployed
across US and EU operations.

**Decision:** conditional go — with the auto-reject feature removed, New York City held pending a
real bias audit, and Ireland held pending vendor documentation.
**The hard call:** rejecting the vendor's bias audit — superseded model version, vendor-commissioned,
not intersectional.
*EU AI Act (post–Digital Omnibus) · NIST AI RMF · ISO/IEC 42001 · NYC LL144 · Colorado SB 24-205 · Illinois HB 3773 · Title VII*

### [2 — ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Statement of Applicability](p2-iso27001-soa/)
All 93 Annex A controls for a fully remote health-data SaaS company. **84 applicable, 9 excluded
with documented rationale.**

**The hard call:** excluding eight physical controls while keeping six — because remote working does
not delete physical risk, it relocates it into sixty homes the company does not control.

### [3 — From Quarterly Spreadsheet to Continuous Control: Privileged Access Review](p3-continuous-access-review/)
A runnable exception engine that turns a 388-row rubber-stamp review into 89 decisions a human can
actually make.

**The hard call:** the metric reported is not the 77% reduction — it is the retain rate, because that
is the number that can embarrass the programme.

### [4 — Risk Acceptance: Unsupported Database Holding 340,000 Records](p4-risk-acceptance/)
A one-page memo an executive signs, written in business language with the trade-offs stated honestly.

**The hard call:** documenting that "we knew and accepted it" is the correct way to make the
decision — and is also the fact that appears in litigation.

### [5 — Vendor Risk in Dollars: FAIR Quantification](p5-tprm-fair/)
A CFO asked whether a payroll vendor was worth keeping. "Amber" is not an input to a budget decision.

**Answer:** $724K annualised loss expectancy today, $165K after $95K of remediation — a 5.9x return.
Bringing it in-house costs $600K and reduces nothing.

## How these were built

Each assessment starts from a realistic scenario, applies current regulation and framework
requirements, and ends in a decision with a named owner and a stated basis. The companies are
constructed; the legal analysis, control design and reasoning are real practice, current as of
September 2026.

Where the law is unsettled, the documents say so rather than inventing certainty. Where an
assumption is doing heavy lifting, it is written down so a reviewer can challenge it.
